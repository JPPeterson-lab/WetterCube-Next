## v0.3.0-beta1 (2026-10-23)

### Fixed
- **Temperature colour:** The temperature was always green, even at 6 degrees. The colour now changes as on the Fusion: below 8 degrees blue, below 15 green, below 24 yellow, above that red. This applies to the current temperature and to the forecast values, in the light and the dark design.
